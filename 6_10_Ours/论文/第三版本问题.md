**Contribution 第四条不应算作贡献**

```
·Evaluation under distribution shifts: Experiments on 
RadioML 2018.01A evaluate in-distribution performance 
and generalization to unseen SNRs and modulation formats.
```

实验设计本身不是方法贡献，把它列为 Contribution 之一会给审稿人"凑贡献点"的印象。建议删掉，或者改写成方法层面的贡献，例如"A three-protocol evaluation framework that separately measures in-distribution performance, SNR generalization, and blind modulation generalization"，但这同样说服力有限。最稳妥的做法是直接删掉这一条，Contribution 保留前三条即可。



### 🟡 实验分析语气仍有推测性表述

Section IV-B(3) 写道：

> *"This result suggests that the proposed framework learns more transferable signal-presence characteristics rather than relying primarily on modulation patterns observed during training."*

"suggests"是推测性语言，在分析自己的实验结果时不够确定。如果结论确实成立，可以改为：

> *"This result demonstrates that the proposed framework captures modulation-agnostic signal-presence characteristics, as evidenced by consistent detection performance across all ten unseen modulation formats."*
